## Overview

WaveShaper is a desktop audio processing studio for Windows. A file passes through one chain of equaliser, effects, dynamics and mastering, which you hear as you adjust it and export when it is right. Every stage can be bypassed and saved as a preset, and metering follows integrated loudness and true peak throughout.

### Equaliser and effects

- 10-band equaliser with all bands, ranges and response types, high- and low-pass filters from 6 to 48 dB/oct, and 44 built-in presets
- Reverb in six types, delay in six modes, saturation in five models, and tremolo, vibrato, chorus, phaser and flanger
- Creative effects, from bitcrush and reverse to stutter, gate, lo-fi, ring modulation and granular texture

### Dynamics and mastering

- Compressor with soft knee, sidechain, lookahead and auto-release, plus gate/expander and brick-wall limiter
- Four-band multiband compression
- Mastering suite: true-peak limiter with five styles, stereo imaging and mid/side balance, exciter and tape saturation, and presets for streaming, broadcast, CD and vinyl
- Time stretch from 0.25x to 4x and pitch shift of ±12 semitones, with five algorithms

### Listening and measurement

- Integrated LUFS, peak and true-peak metering, and a spectrum analyser with seven display modes
- Player with eight real-time visualisations, crossfade and mono check
- Output through WASAPI shared or exclusive, DirectSound or ASIO, with adjustable buffer size and dithering

### Export

- WAV, FLAC, MP3 up to 320 kbps and OGG Vorbis, and AAC in Premium
- Peak normalisation, and LUFS normalisation for streaming (−14), Apple (−16) and broadcast (−23) in Premium
- Up to 48 kHz and 24-bit in Basic; up to 192 kHz and 32-bit float in Premium

## Installation and updates

1. Download the installer from the [latest release](https://github.com/Arctisoft-Studio/WaveShaper-Audio-Processing-Studio/releases/latest).
2. Run it and follow the setup. It installs for your Windows user account, so no administrator rights are needed. Setup can also install Studio Hub alongside it.
3. WaveShaper checks for new versions on the update channel you choose: Stable, Preview or Manual.

Updates are taken only from this repository's official releases. Before an update runs, its installer is checked against the size and SHA-256 digest that GitHub records for the release, and an installation signed by Arctisoft Studio accepts only an installer from the same publisher. WaveShaper can always update by itself; [Studio Hub](https://github.com/Arctisoft-Studio/Arctisoft-Studio-Hub) offers the same releases from one place.

## Account and privacy

All processing happens on your computer, and your audio files never leave it. WaveShaper works without an account. Signing in with your Arctisoft account adds Premium, the trial and, if you choose, settings sync across computers; presets sync with Premium.
